# Books: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

## The short version

Books works fully offline. It has no internet permission, collects nothing and sends nothing anywhere. Everything it remembers stays on your phone.

## Who is responsible

Tintako, Croatia · app@tintako.com

## What the app stores, and where

Only on your phone, in the app's private storage:

- the list of books it found (file fingerprint, title, author, file name and location)
- small cover pictures
- text converted from TXT, HTML, FB2 and ODT so it can be read
- a copy of a book you open from another app (for example a file manager), because access to the original ends when the app closes
- your reading position, the percentage read and your bookmarks
- your settings (language, font, order of the library, chosen folders)

Your books are only read. The app never changes or deletes them, and copies only a book you open from another app. Removing the app deletes everything it stored, but not your books.

## Permissions, and what each is for

Books asks for no permissions. When another app opens a book in Books, Android gives access to that one file only. You choose your book folder once in the system file picker, and the app then has read-only access to that one folder. The APK contains one internal Android permission (`DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION`) that gives no access to anything on the phone.

## The Tintako Launcher card

If you use Tintako Launcher, Books gives it a card with your last book: title, author, page, percentage and cover. Only Tintako Launcher, checked by its signing key, can read the card, and it stays on your phone.

## What leaves, and only when you ask

Nothing leaves on its own. A backup file exists only if you export one, and it is saved where you choose.

## Who else receives anything

No one. The app connects to no server. The Tip jar shows a QR code for ko-fi.com/tintako; the app itself never opens that address.

## Legal basis (EU/EEA and UK)

Tintako processes no personal data about you, so no legal basis is needed. Everything the app stores stays on your device under your control.

## Your rights

Because Tintako receives no data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You can delete everything the app stored by removing the app or clearing its data.

## Children

The app is suitable for everyone and collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published here and in the app (Settings → About → Privacy).
